### Video 1: The Hero Terminal (10s)
**Format:** Landscape (16:9 aspect ratio)

The terminal window has no traditional OS buttons, just pure frosted glass aesthetics with dark charcoal text. Text rapidly types out "npm install malicious-pkg". Suddenly, a sleek, glowing cyan border flashes around the terminal. A crisp, modern popup slides in reading "Blocked by Agentinel". The text self-corrects to "npm install safe-pkg". The camera slowly pans and tilts around the 3D floating terminal throughout the 10 seconds. Ultra-smooth, premium tech commercial aesthetic.

Keep the contrast high with pure white negative space.

**Format:** Landscape (16:9 aspect ratio)
